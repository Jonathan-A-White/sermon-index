# The next sources

Three modern catalogues, in the order the recipe ([adding-a-source.md](adding-a-source.md)) would take them, with their terms quoted and a recommendation each. Read on 2026-11-05 (mw-hxztgfo.8): for each, only its `robots.txt` and its terms or copyright pages (and, for SermonAudio, the one news page that says where an API key comes from). No sermon page, passage page or API call was fetched. None of this is legal advice or a licence: what is quoted is what the pages say.

## Which first: Truth For Life

**File Truth For Life first.** It is the only one of the three where a script may read the catalogue (its `robots.txt` allows it, and nothing in its terms forbids automated access), it has a page per passage that gives the passage as a printed label, and it is free. Its one open question is a small one the Governor can answer in a word: the Policies page does not say whether an index of titles, passages and addresses may be kept. So the story's first step is that question, with the quoted words, and the tool is written after the answer. Ligonier should not be filed (its terms forbid the tool), and SermonAudio waits for the Governor (a key, terms unread).

## Truth For Life (`www.truthforlife.org`)

- **The catalogue**: a page per passage, `https://www.truthforlife.org/bible/Romans 5:8/`, each sermon with its text as a printed label, a date, a duration and an address `/resources/sermon/<slug>/`; "Browse by Scripture" in the menu; a sitemap.
- **`robots.txt`** (`https://www.truthforlife.org/robots.txt`): `User-agent: *`, `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=yes, ai-input=yes`; it disallows query forms only (`*?month=`, `*?year=`, `*?topic=`, `*?scripture=`, `*?type=`, `*?sort=`, `*?chapter=`, `*?per_page=`, `*?page=`, `*?q=` and the `&` forms) and `/blog/flashdrive/`, `/flashdrive/`; `Disallow: /` for YandexBot; `Crawl-delay: 1` for Swiftbot; `Sitemap: https://www.truthforlife.org/sitemap.xml`.
- **The terms**, `https://www.truthforlife.org/about/policies/` ("Policies"; there is no `/terms`): "You may duplicate and distribute copies of Alistair’s sermons without prior permission as long as you: Do not alter or edit the content in any way; Do not charge for the material (even to recover your fee)". "If you would like to include a link to an audio or video clip on your site, you may embed a link to the file on our site. You must include a link back to our site at www.truthforlife.org." "We reserve the right to revoke or modify these permissions at any time." "Truth For Life and Alistair Begg reserve all copyright protections under applicable law." Web content may be posted "as long as you: Do not edit the content in any way; Include a link to the original article and our homepage ... Provide the appropriate credit for the content: “Copyright Truth For Life. Used with Permission. www.truthforlife.org.”"
- **Not read**: the syndication terms (`/syndicate/instructions/`, "Content Use Terms and Conditions", behind an account request). The Policies page says nothing about an index of facts and nothing about automated reading.
- **Recommendation**: add it, link-out only, as a tool `tools/truthforlife` walking the passage pages at one request a second (never a query form), after the Governor says that an index of titles, passages and addresses is acceptable on the strength of the Policies page's invitation to link (or after a line from the ministry). `on_by_default: false` to start. A sermon labelled `Acts 1:1 – Jude 1:25` is a `series` row. Credit: "Alistair Begg, Truth For Life (truthforlife.org)". Size: M (the sitemap would say how many sermons; unread).

## Ligonier Ministries (`learn.ligonier.org`, `www.ligonier.org`)

- **The catalogue**: a Scripture Index with a page per book (`/scripture/romans`); the chapter is a filter in the page, not an address (`/scripture/romans/5` is a 404, from the earlier probe). The sitemaps named in `learn.ligonier.org/robots.txt` include `sitemap-scripture.xml`, `sitemap-sermons.xml` and `sitemap-series.xml`.
- **`robots.txt`** (`https://learn.ligonier.org/robots.txt`): `User-agent: *`, `Allow: *`, `Disallow: /api`, `Disallow: /auth`, and the sitemaps. (`https://www.ligonier.org/robots.txt`: `Disallow: /api`, `/images/`, `/javascripts/`, `/stylesheets/`, `/typefaces/`.)
- **The terms**, `https://www.ligonier.org/terms` ("Effective as of November 3, 2026"): "You may not (1) modify, copy, reproduce, republish, upload, post, transmit, distribute, publicly display, or create derivative works from Ligonier Services content except as permitted by these Terms or our Copyright Policy; (2) use the Ligonier Services or its content for any commercial purpose; ... or (7) use any robot, scraper, crawler, or other automated means to access the Ligonier Services for any purpose without our express written permission." And `https://www.ligonier.org/copyright-policy`: "To share free audio and video content from Ligonier.org, you must hyperlink to the page that contains the media. Content may not be re-uploaded or distributed in other ways without special permission from Ligonier." "No derivative work may be generated".
- **Recommendation: do not file it.** The `robots.txt` allows reading, but the terms forbid, for any purpose, any "robot, scraper, crawler, or other automated means" without express written permission, so a tool is out, and rows written by hand from a script-free reading are an index of the site's compilation ("the compilation and arrangement of content" is stated to be theirs) that the terms do not allow without permission either. It becomes a story only if the Governor asks Ligonier for written permission and gets it. A link to a page a person found is another matter.

## SermonAudio (`www.sermonaudio.com`)

- **The catalogue**: an API, `GET /v2/node/sermons` with `book` (an OSIS specifier), `chapter`, `chapterEnd`, `verse`, `verseEnd` (the 2026-11-05 probe read `https://api.sermonaudio.com/v2/openapi.json`); the passage is a machine-read OSIS range, the best of any catalogue here.
- **`robots.txt`** (`https://www.sermonaudio.com/robots.txt`): a block headed "Block AI and scraping bots" with `Disallow: /` for named agents including `ClaudeBot`, `Claude-User`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `GPTBot` and `CCBot`; `User-agent: *` with `Crawl-delay: 5` and `Disallow` of `/admin/`, `/dashboard/`, `/player/`, `/sign-in/`, `/solo/` and a few others; a sitemap, `https://www.sermonaudio.com/sitemap/index.xml`.
- **The key**, read on the news page `https://www.sermonaudio.com/news/api` (26 March 2024): "An API Key is necessary to access your content and upload sermons via the v2 APIs. Every broadcaster account automatically has an API Key assigned and is viewable in the Settings section of the Dashboard." So the key is issued **to a broadcaster account** (a church or ministry that publishes on the site) and is described as the way to reach "your content"; whether a key reads other broadcasters' sermons by passage, whether an account is free, and what the key's terms allow are **not stated on any page read**. The API's Terms of Service and Programming Guide are drawn by script on `https://api.sermonaudio.com/` and were not retrievable (the earlier probe too). The site calls itself "the largest and most trusted library with millions of free audio and video sermons", which is about listeners, not about developers.
- **Recommendation: the Governor's, and not yet.** It needs a key, which is his to obtain (the Builder makes no account and accepts no terms for him); the terms are unread; and its `robots.txt` names this project's kind of reader. If he wants it: he reads the API's terms in a browser, says whether a broadcaster account is free and whether it may be used for an index of other broadcasters' sermons, and puts the key in the Laptop's environment file; the story then writes `tools/sermonaudio` against the API (`delay_ms = 5000` in `host_rules`, the key never committed).
