## Read from the opening paragraphs (mw-qtu3zvz.131)

- `1~299858` *The New Birth: A Discourse*, translated from the French: John 3:3, the verse it opens on.
- `2~620438` to `2~691533`, *Brief Outlines of Sermons*, Outline I to XXX: each prints its text, in quotation marks and then its address, under the heading ("Outline I" is on Genesis 1:26). The index titles each "Outline N: <its text>", since "Outline I" alone says nothing in a list of sermons.
- The book prints two of the addresses wrongly, over the verses it quotes. Outline VIII reads "Jeremiah 17:19" over "The heart is deceitful above all things" (Jeremiah 17:9), and Outline XXIV "Amos 6:12" over "Prepare to meet thy God" (Amos 4:12). The index takes the verses quoted, Jeremiah 17:9 and Amos 4:12.
- Outline XVI cites Romans 16:25, a verse the book table in this repository does not hold (it gives the chapter 24 verses); that one cited passage is refused by the importer and named in the source's REPORT.
