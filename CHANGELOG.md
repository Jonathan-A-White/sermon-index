- 2026-11-05: Adding a preacher is a data add, written down. `docs/adding-a-source.md` is the recipe a Builder session follows (find the catalogue, read its terms and `robots.txt` first and quote them, link-out only, write the files, `sermons check` and `--urls`, load, credit, and the places outside this repo a new source touches), with Truth For Life as the worked example; `docs/candidates.md` names the next three catalogues with their terms quoted (Truth For Life first; Ligonier's terms forbid a script; SermonAudio needs a key); `prompts/add-source.md` is the story template and `sermons propose <dir>` prints it filled from a `source.toml` and its row count; `sources/_template` gains a README and every new field. `sermons check` now **warns** (exit status unchanged) of a row whose `ref` spans more than 3 chapters; a row may be marked `"series": true`, which keeps it, lists it by its source and finds it by search, but loads it with no passage, so no passage query ever matches it. A row may carry `expect_title` and `source.toml` a `[host_rules."<host>"]` table (`title_in_page`, `delay_ms`) for `check --urls`: CCEL's title test is now the built-in rule of that table. No migration, and no existing source changes.

- 2026-11-05: The theme tier. `GET /v1/sermons?ref=rom.5.8&tier=theme` answers `{ref, passage, theme}`: the passage rows as before, then the sermons whose preaching text shares a topic with the ref (a topic of at most 300 verses, `store.ThemeMaxVerses`), each once with its narrowest topic named in `theme: {source, topic, label, part, part_label}` and never repeating a passage row; `tier=theme-only` leaves out the passage rows (`"passage": null`), `theme=<source:topic>` restricts to a topic, and `limit` and `offset` apply to each tier. Without `tier` the answer is unchanged. `GET /v1/themes?ref=` rows gain `sermons` (sermons on the topic by preaching text) and `verses` (the topic's width). Migration `0003_theme_verses` adds `themes.verses`: **run `sermons migrate` and then `sermons load --all sources/`**, or the theme tier finds no topic. Over the real files Romans 5:8's theme tier is 364 sermons (Thompson's two topics alone 74, Torrey's five alone 323). docs/api.md, "The theme tier".
